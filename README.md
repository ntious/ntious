## Research

I develop and evaluate **explainable, efficient, and reliable machine-learning systems**. My recent research focuses on cybersecurity, examining how **changing conditions, limited resources, and incomplete evidence** affect predictions and decisions. I also study explainable decision support in healthcare and education.

My current work is developing around three connected research questions:

### Reliable explanations under changing conditions

**How reliable are AI predictions and explanations when operating conditions change?**

I study explainability, distribution shift, open-set evaluation, adversarial conditions, and cross-dataset generalization, particularly in malware, ransomware, and intrusion-detection systems.

### Efficient and robust learning

**How do resource constraints and model simplification affect predictive and explanatory reliability?**

My work investigates feature refinement, sparsity, lightweight neural architectures, edge inference, and performance–efficiency–robustness trade-offs.

### Evidence-aware decision systems

**When should an AI system predict, abstain, defer, or require stronger evidence?**

This emerging direction investigates evidence sufficiency and integrity, selective prediction, uncertainty-aware decision-making, and human oversight in consequential AI systems.

## Emerging work: evidence-gated AI

My recent work explores **evidence-gated decision systems**: AI systems that assess whether available evidence is adequate for prediction rather than treating every input as equally decision-ready.

### Education
